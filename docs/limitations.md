# Remaining limits after native GPU development acceptance

- This is PhysX simulation, not a real-hardware experiment. The track changes Franka end-effector configurations; it does not establish arbitrary-arm generalization or isolate hardware-only gripper effects. Native self-collision/drive/stand differences are recorded in `evidence/gpu/embodiment_diff.json`.
- The native runtime, both-body reset/step, 10 nominal grasps per body, TCP/gripper checks, spawn-time mass/inertia/material readback and force-response diagnostics have real evidence. They do not certify a full formal pilot.
- Both-body low/mid/high sensitivity measurements are development diagnostics. Low grasp height can hit the table; higher requested lift velocities can saturate in actual tracking. Common feasible bounds and task difficulty still need review before freezing. Initial performance currently shows saturation; the baseline/judge must not be weakened to force a gain.
- Physical toss/release diagnostics reject false success for both bodies; CPU tests cover exact hold-time boundaries and reset. No validated two-finger contact-topology sensor is claimed.
- The production runner builds a fresh scene per episode. This isolates dynamics but is slow. Persistent mass setters failed a diagnostic and are not used as a substitute. A full 100-episode throughput study and any optimized reset path still require verification.
- Source acquisition, strict budgets, reconstruction and rollback have a real 20-episode development run. Its candidate was rolled back. A Kimi K3 5-episode adaptation also retained the initial skill. These are not positive transfer findings.
- The paired development bridge tests equivalent-source aliases on small target samples. Formal source audits, all 0/5/20 matrices, both directions, independent complete seed packages, pair-level missing-data handling across the full pilot, resume and complete formal reporting are pending.
- Workspace/actual joint-limit failure classification, comprehensive infrastructure-cost accounting and terminal-trace/report validation need further work before outside submissions. Current local learner isolation is an allowlist interface, not a malicious-code security boundary.
- Kimi K3 is identified by provider/model ID; the service exposes no immutable weight revision. Calls are stochastic at the required temperature 1. Usage, repair and fallback are audited; 1M context is provider-advertised and has not been stress-tested. Benchmark requests remain bounded to 6,000 input / 800 output tokens.
- Upstream native dependency overrides produce metadata-check exceptions, including an ONNX CPU/GPU metadata mismatch. They have not broken the exercised state/IK/PhysX path, but formal dependency exception review is incomplete and the freeze gate rejects it. Optional ONNX/OpenPI/Newton/search paths remain unverified.
- No test distribution is frozen, asset dependency checksums are not yet complete, and no official research conclusion or v0.1 acceptance release is claimed. Historical setup/physical diagnostic failures remain in the evidence trail.
